=== Indianapolis Motor Speedway Efficiency Strategy ===

Objective: minimum predicted electrical energy subject to the lap-time,
speed-change, motor, and fuse constraints.

Reference track length: 3798.9 m
Lap-time constraint: 550.0 s
Predicted lap time: 549.2 s
Predicted lap energy: 43046.9 J
Predicted energy intensity: 11331.4 J/km
Target speed range: 19.0-28.0 km/h
Mean segment target: 25.0 km/h
Accelerate/hold/coast segments: 26/0/50

Training model: Indianapolis afternoon run (same-track fit)
Training runs/laps/samples: 1/3/2683
The fit omits absolute track position.

The model underpredicted energy by 14.8% when replayed against the held-out
Indy morning run (106.1 kJ predicted vs 124.6 kJ measured at 50 m aggregation).
This checks energy on an existing drive. The optimized lap, including its
predicted current and energy, still needs on-track validation.

Training sources:
- afternoon-run: 3 laps, 2683 merged samples

Centerline GPS: morning-run/Utsm.gpx and afternoon-run/Utsm-2.gpx, all
three detected laps from each run. Aligned 720-point closed resamples,
combined per-run median paths, applied 12 m circular smoothing and
resampled at approximately 5 m. Origin aligned to the first firmware
map coordinate. The 50 m optimization segments, 19 km/h starting
speed and 28 km/h cap follow the firmware map settings or values.
The 550 s lap limit approximates its 546 s mean lap at target speeds.
This closed-loop plan can differ from the firmware's three-lap profile.
